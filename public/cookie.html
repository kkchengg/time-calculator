Cookie Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

This page lists the cookies and local storage used by Time calculator and by the Google AdSense advertising it displays.

Cookies set by Google AdSense

Advertising is served by Google. The exact cookies set can vary over time and by region; the following are the most common.

Name Provider Purpose Type
__gads Google Ad delivery and frequency capping. Persistent
__gpi Google Advertising and measurement. Persistent
test_cookie Google Checks whether the browser accepts cookies. Short-lived

Local storage set by this site

Name Purpose Type
time-calculator-locale Remembers your language preference. Persistent (local storage)

Managing cookies

Visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland choose whether to allow personalised advertising through Google's certified consent message. You can change that choice at any time using the Cookie settings link in the footer of the app. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Contact

Questions can be raised at github.com/kkchengg/time-calculator/issues.
