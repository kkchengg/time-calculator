Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Time calculator is a free, browser-based tool. This page explains what data is processed when you use it and the choices you have.

Calculations run in your browser

The times you enter are processed entirely on your device. They are not sent to, stored on, or logged by our servers, and we cannot see them.

Local storage

We store a single preference in your browser's local storage: time-calculator-locale, which remembers the language you selected (English, Traditional Chinese or Simplified Chinese). It stays on your device and can be cleared at any time through your browser settings.

Advertising

This site is supported by advertising delivered by Google AdSense (publisher ID ca-pub-6689056038494624). Google and its partners may use cookies and similar identifiers to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Ads settings and opt-out: google.com/settings/ads
  • Industry opt-out: aboutads.info/choices

Visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are shown a consent message provided by Google's certified consent management platform before personalised advertising cookies are used. You can change your choice at any time with the Cookie settings link in the footer of the app.

Analytics

This site does not run its own analytics or tracking scripts.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised at github.com/kkchengg/time-calculator/issues.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the latest revision.
